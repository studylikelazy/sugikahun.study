# Assets

**Art direction:** A clean, approachable 3D market-exchange lounge for teen and young-adult players. Use deep ink navy for the environment, cool blue for terminal infrastructure, mint for positive movement, coral for negative movement, and amber only for archive collectibles. Use simple geometric props, crisp HUDs, a friendly original blocky avatar, and an isometric third-person camera. Do not use Roblox logos, copyrighted characters, or copied interfaces.
